Where listings come from:

  1. Get an eBay developer account and a production keyset at developer.ebay.com.
  2. Fill in eBay's marketplace account deletion settings — production keys stay locked until you do. Your worker URL answers that check for you.
  3. Deploy ebay-relay-worker.js (shipped with this file) at dash.cloudflare.com under Workers.
  4. Add the secrets EBAY_APP_ID, EBAY_CERT_ID, RELAY_KEY, and paste the worker URL above.

eBay sends no CORS headers, so the browser cannot call it without something in between. A public proxy sees your token. Use this to test, then move to the relay.

Value estimates

Estimates use the median of comparable listings, not a plain average — one fantasy Buy It Now at 40× the real level would drag a mean up with it. Sold prices are what coins actually fetched; eBay gates those behind its Marketplace Insights API, which is a Limited Release restricted to approved partners. Without it, estimates come from current asking prices, which run higher than realised prices and are shown in gold to keep the difference visible.
